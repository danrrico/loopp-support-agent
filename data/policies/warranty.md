# Warranty Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## 2-Year Limited Warranty

Every Northwind Outfitters product is covered by a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase — longer coverage than most retailers offer. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This includes stitching failures, zipper defects, seam leaks in waterproof gear, and hardware failures such as broken buckles or pole sections.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or accidents, fading from sun exposure, or damage caused by unauthorized modifications or repairs. Cosmetic damage that does not affect function is not covered.

## Filing a Warranty Claim

To file a claim, contact support with your order number, photos of the defect, and a short description of the problem. We review claims within 5 business days. If your claim is approved, we will repair the item, replace it with the same model, or offer a store credit for the purchase price if the model is no longer available.

You are responsible for shipping the item to us for inspection unless the item is under $50, in which case we cover return shipping. Approved repairs take 10 to 15 business days from the day we receive your item.

## Warranty on Repaired and Replaced Items

Repaired items carry a 90-day warranty on the repair work. Replacement items are covered for the remainder of the original 2-year warranty period, or 90 days, whichever is longer. A replacement does not restart the 2-year warranty period.

## Extended Care for Tents and Footwear

Tents and footwear include free seam-sealing and re-waterproofing advice for the life of the product. Replacement parts such as tent poles, stakes, and boot laces are available at cost for 5 years after purchase. Contact support with your product model to order parts.

## What Voids the Warranty

The warranty is void if the product was damaged by misuse, such as using a 3-season tent in winter storm conditions, overloading a backpack beyond its rated capacity, or using footwear for a sport it was not designed for. Unauthorized repairs or modifications, including third-party seam sealing with non-approved products, also void the warranty. Normal wear, including scuffed soles and faded fabric, is not a defect and is not covered.
