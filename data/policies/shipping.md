# Shipping Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and is free on all orders over $75. Orders under $75 pay a flat $6.95 for standard shipping. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time ship the same business day. Orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day. We ship Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

Every order includes tracking. You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships, and you can follow it from your account page.

## Express and Overnight Options

Express shipping arrives in 2 business days and costs $14.95 per order. Overnight shipping arrives the next business day and costs $24.95 per order. Express and overnight orders must be placed before 2 PM Eastern Time to ship the same day. These options are available for all items except oversized gear such as canoes and large tents, which ship by freight.

## Delivery Areas and Restrictions

We ship to all 50 US states and to APO/FPO addresses. We do not currently ship internationally. Deliveries to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories take 5 to 10 business days for standard shipping. A signature is required on delivery for orders over $300.

## Lost or Delayed Packages

If your tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or your package is marked delivered but you have not received it, contact us. We will open a carrier investigation, which usually takes 3 to 5 business days. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we will reship your order or issue a full refund.

## International Shipping

We do not currently ship outside the United States, including Canada and Mexico. We are working on international shipping and expect to offer it in the future. If you are outside the US, you can use a package-forwarding service, but we cannot guarantee delivery times or cover forwarding costs, and returns from outside the US are not eligible for prepaid return labels.
