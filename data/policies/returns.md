# Returns Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## 60-Day Return Window

You can return any unused item within 60 days of the delivery date for a full refund to your original payment method. The item must be in its original condition, with tags attached and all included accessories. Footwear must be tried on indoors only; boots with worn soles are not eligible for return.

To start a return, sign in to your account and open the order, then select the items you want to return — for example, to return a jacket, open the order that contains it and select the jacket. You can print a prepaid return label from your account page. Drop the package at any carrier location listed on the label.

## Refund Timing

Once your return arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after inspection. Your bank may take an additional 1 to 2 business days to post the credit. Refunds are always issued for the full purchase price of the returned item, including any discount applied at checkout.

If you paid with a gift card, the refund is issued as a new gift card for the same amount. Shipping charges from the original order are not refunded unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.

## Defective or Damaged Items

If your item arrives defective or damaged, contact us within 30 days of delivery. We will cover the return shipping and offer a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You do not need to return a defective item before we ship a replacement for orders under $200. For orders over $200, we ask you to return the item first so our team can inspect it.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

The following items cannot be returned for hygiene or safety reasons: underwear, socks, swimwear, water bottles, and food items. Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page cannot be returned. Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded. If you are unsure whether an item is eligible, contact support before shipping it back.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or color within the same 60-day window. Start the exchange from your account page, just like a return, and select "Exchange" instead of "Refund". We ship the replacement as soon as your return is scanned by the carrier, so you do not wait for the full inspection. Exchanges are free — there is no extra shipping charge in either direction. If the replacement costs more than the original item, you pay the difference at checkout; if it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method.
