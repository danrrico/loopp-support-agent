# Account Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## Creating and Managing Your Account

You can shop as a guest, but creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, print return labels, and see your order history. To create an account, provide an email address and a password of at least 8 characters. You must be 18 years or older to create an account.

You can update your name, email address, shipping addresses, and payment methods at any time from your account page. Changing your email address requires confirming the new address by email.

## Password Resets

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. We will email you a reset link that expires in 24 hours. For security, reset links can only be used once. If you do not receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Closing Your Account

You can close your account at any time from your account settings. Closing your account cancels any pending orders that have not yet shipped and forfeits any unused store credit. Order history is retained for 7 years for tax and legal purposes, even after your account is closed. To request a copy of your personal data before closing your account, contact support; we provide data exports within 30 days.

## Account Security

We will never ask for your password by email or phone. If you suspect someone has accessed your account, change your password immediately and contact support. We may temporarily lock your account after 5 failed sign-in attempts; the lock clears automatically after 30 minutes, or you can reset your password to unlock it right away.

## Notifications and Marketing Emails

Order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications are always sent by email and cannot be turned off while you have an active order. Marketing emails about sales and new products are optional — you can unsubscribe from them at any time with the link at the bottom of any marketing email, or from your notification settings. Unsubscribing from marketing does not affect order or account emails.
