# Billing Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## How Charges Appear

Charges from Northwind Outfitters appear on your statement as "Northwind Outfitters" or "NW Outfitters". Your card is charged when your order ships, not when you place it. For pre-orders, your card is charged when the item ships. If your order ships in multiple packages, you may see separate charges for each shipment.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept checks, money orders, or cash on delivery.

## Refunds and Credits

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after we approve your return or warranty claim. If you paid with PayPal, the refund goes to your PayPal balance. Store credits are issued as a code by email and never expire. Gift card refunds are issued as a new gift card for the same amount.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us within 60 days of the charge. We will investigate and, if the charge was our error, refund it in full within 5 business days.

## Price Adjustments

If the price of an item you purchased drops within 14 days of your order date, we will refund the difference. Contact support with your order number. Price adjustments do not apply to sale items, clearance items, or purchases made with a coupon code. Only one price adjustment is allowed per item.

## Taxes and Duties

Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address and the tax rate in effect at the time of purchase. We collect sales tax in all states where we are required to do so. There are no additional duties or customs fees for orders shipped within the United States.

## Failed Payments

If your card is declined at checkout, your order is held for 48 hours while you update your payment method. We will email you a link to retry the payment. After 48 hours without a successful payment, the order is automatically cancelled and the items return to stock. A declined payment never results in a charge. If your card was charged and the order still shows as unpaid, contact us and we will resolve it within 2 business days.
