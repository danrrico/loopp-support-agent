# Orders Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## Changing or Cancelling an Order

You can change the shipping address or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it, as long as it has not yet shipped. Sign in to your account, open the order, and select "Modify" or "Cancel". After 2 hours, or once the order has shipped, changes are no longer possible and you will need to use the return process instead.

If you need to change the items in your order, cancel the order within the 2-hour window and place a new one. We cannot swap items on an order that is already being packed.

## Order Status and Tracking

Order statuses are: Processing (we are preparing your order), Shipped (your order is with the carrier), Delivered, Cancelled, and Backordered. You can check your order status at any time from your account page. Tracking numbers are emailed when your order ships and appear on the order details page.

## Backorders

If an item is backordered, we will tell you the expected ship date before you complete your purchase. Backordered items usually ship within 14 days. You can cancel a backordered item at any time before it ships for a full refund. If a backorder is delayed by more than 30 days, we will email you and offer the option to cancel or wait.

## Split Shipments

If your order contains items from different warehouses, it may ship in multiple packages at no extra cost to you. Each package has its own tracking number. Your card is charged per shipment as each package leaves our warehouse.

## Order Confirmation

After you place an order, you will receive a confirmation email within 5 minutes with your order number. Order numbers start with "NW-" followed by 5 digits, for example NW-88412. Keep your order number handy when contacting support — it is the fastest way for us to find your order. If you do not receive a confirmation email, check your spam folder or sign in to your account to see the order.
