# Pricing Policy — Northwind Outfitters

## How Prices Are Set

Our prices include all standard features for each product. Prices shown on the website are in US dollars and do not include sales tax, which is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address. Prices may change at any time; the price at the time you place your order is the price you pay.

## Price Match

We match the advertised price of an identical in-stock item from any authorized US retailer within 14 days of your purchase. Contact support with a link to the competitor's listing. Price matches do not apply to sale items, clearance items, marketplace sellers, auction listings, or prices that include a coupon or membership discount. We will refund the difference to your original payment method within 5 business days of approval.

## Discounts and Coupons

We offer a 10% student discount with a valid student ID, verified once per year. Military members and first responders receive a 15% discount with verification. Only one discount code can be used per order. Discounts cannot be combined with price-match adjustments, and they do not apply to gift cards or shipping charges.

Sale items are final sale for price-match purposes, but they can still be returned within the normal 60-day return window. Clearance items marked "Final Sale" on the product page cannot be returned.

## Gift Cards

Gift cards are available in amounts from $10 to $500. They never expire and have no fees. Gift cards can be used for any product, including sale items, but cannot be used to buy other gift cards. If your order total is less than your gift card balance, the remaining balance stays on the card.

## Clearance and Outlet

Clearance items are discounted 30% to 70% and are marked with an orange "Clearance" tag. Clearance prices are final and cannot be combined with discount codes or price-match adjustments. Clearance items can be returned within the normal 60-day window unless the product page says "Final Sale". Outlet items are previous-season models in new condition and carry the full 2-year warranty.
